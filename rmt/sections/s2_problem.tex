\section{Problem Setup}\label{sec:problem}

The long-only mean-variance portfolio solves
\[
  \min_{w}\; w^\top \Sigma_0\, w - \rho\,\hat\mu^\top w
  \quad\text{subject to}\quad
  Bw = c,\quad w \geq 0\,,
\]
where $\rho \geq 0$ is a return-tilt weight, $\hat\mu$ is a vector of expected
returns, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times n}$ of full row rank and $c \in \mathbb{R}^p$
carry $p \ll n$ linear balance constraints, and $\Sigma_0$ is an estimate of the
return covariance. We take that estimate in the rescaled form
\[
  \Sigma_0 \;=\; D^{1/2}\, C_0\, D^{1/2}\,,
\]
where $D = \mathrm{diag}(\hat\Sigma)$ holds the sample asset variances and
$C_0 \succ 0$ is a cleaned correlation. Section~\ref{sec:rmt} constructs the
particular $C_0$ used here by random-matrix cleaning; the reduction of this
section holds for any $C_0 \succ 0$.

The fully-invested budget constraint $\mathbf{1}^\top w = 1$ is the case $p = 1$,
$B = \mathbf{1}^\top$, $c = 1$; it is the leading case throughout, and the reader
may substitute it wherever a balance system is not written out. General $B$
additionally accommodates group budgets (sector sleeves summing to prescribed
weights) and factor neutralities $\beta^\top w = 0$ at the same cost structure.

\paragraph{Standardised coordinates}
Because $D^{1/2}$ is positive-diagonal, $\Sigma_0 \succ 0$ whenever
$C_0 \succ 0$, and the change of variables $y = D^{1/2} w$ is exact and preserves
the sign constraint ($w \geq 0 \Leftrightarrow y \geq 0$). Since
$w^\top \Sigma_0 w = y^\top C_0 y$, the problem becomes
\[
  \min_{y}\; y^\top C_0\, y - \rho\,\tilde\mu^\top y
  \quad\text{subject to}\quad
  B D^{-1/2}\, y = c,\quad y \geq 0\,,
  \qquad \tilde\mu = D^{-1/2}\hat\mu\,,
\]
with the portfolio recovered as $w = D^{-1/2} y$. The solve therefore runs
against the cleaned \emph{correlation} $C_0$ rather than the rebuilt covariance
$\Sigma_0$. This is not cosmetic: the two are conditioned very differently
(Remark~\ref{rem:kappa}), and it is $C_0$, not $\Sigma_0$, that carries the
scalar-identity-plus-low-rank structure the solver of
Section~\ref{sec:algorithm} exploits.

In these coordinates the problem is the equality-augmented non-negative
quadratic of the companion paper~\cite{schmelzer2026nncg}: the balance
constraints $B D^{-1/2} y = c$ are eliminated analytically through a
$p \times p$ Schur complement and $y \geq 0$ is enforced by a primal-dual
active-set outer loop, each step of which re-solves the balance-constrained
subproblem on the current active set. Section~\ref{sec:algorithm} records that
instantiation and develops the Woodbury inner solve that is this paper's
contribution.
